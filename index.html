GAME DESIGN & CORE IDEAS

Where Bullet-Heaven Meets Active Skill Combat

Unlike passive auto-shooters where you only walk in circles, Strike of Destiny fuses relentless roguelike horde escalation with active twin-stick skill expression, projectile deflection physics, and tactical boss encounters.

Active Deflection & Clashing

Your manually aimed projectiles and orbiting sawblades physically clash with enemy fire in mid-air! Deflect Skeleton Archer arrows straight back into the undead ranks—while weaving through unstoppable World Boss bullet-hell patterns.

I-Frame Dodge Rolls & Ultimates

Time your Dodge Roll (Spacebar) for invulnerability frames through lethal shockwaves and gravity wells. Build your Ultimate Gauge to 100% and press Q to unleash class-defining screen clears.

Bloodlust Frenzy & Crisis Events

Chaining rapid kills ignites a Bloodlust Kill Streak that supercharges movement and attack speed. Mid-round Crisis Events—from Blood Moons to fleeing Golden Scavengers—keep every minute unpredictable.

Village Camp Meta-Progression

Every coin looted from slain elites, broken barrels, and treasure chests persists back to the Village Camp. Visit the Blacksmith, Apothecary, and Elder Shrine to permanently empower your heroes across runs.

MODE 01

Standard Campaign

Battle through 3 story acts + a hidden Secret Domain. Survive until the 02:00 Horde Surge, endure the 60-second climax, and slay the stage's World Boss to claim victory.

MODE 02

Endless Survival

No time limit, no mercy. Waves scale infinitely in density and toughness while a new World Boss invades the arena every 2 minutes. Compete for the highest survival time.

MODE 03

Boss Rush Gauntlet

Enter a high-stakes boss gauntlet facing all 4 World Bosses back-to-back. Draft a permanent Boss Relic between rounds and receive a +100 HP breather heal after each victory.

    MASTER THE ARENA

    Controls & Interactive Arena Props

    Keyboard & Mouse Controls

    WASD
    8-Directional Movement

    Also supports Arrow Keys. Smooth wall-sliding around trees, statues, and fences.

    Left Click
    Manual Aimed Attack

    Fires your hero's primary weapon toward the mouse cursor (pairs with automatic firing).

    Spacebar / Shift
    Tactical Dodge Roll

    Grants invulnerability frames (i-frames) to roll safely through boss arrows and shockwaves.

    Q / Right Click
    Class Ultimate Ability

    Unleashes your hero's signature Ultimate ability once the Ultimate Gauge reaches 100%.

    Interactive Battlefield Props & Pickups

    Golden Treasure Chests

    Dropped by Elite Champions and the Golden Scavenger. Grants instant upgrades and gold showers.

    Explosive Powder Barrels

    Lure zombie packs near volatile red barrels and detonate them for massive chain-reaction blasts.

    Sanctified Healing Fountains

    Restores lost vitality during intense horde waves.

    Cursed Blood Altars & Power Pickups

    Activate high-risk shrines and collect Vacuum Magnets, Nuke Bombs, and Potions.
